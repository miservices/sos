Registering a business

Every business in Michigan registers with the Division of Business and Occupational Licensing. You file online in SOS e-Services, the Division reviews it, and your business is added to the public record once it is approved.

Before you start

You will need this information. You can only submit once every section is complete.

SectionWhat you provide
Business informationBusiness name, trade name (DBA) if you use one, business type, a description of what the business does, and its industry.
Business addressThe principal business address: street, city and ZIP code. The state is always Michigan. You can also add a website and a Discord server invite link.
OwnersFor every owner: full legal name, date of birth, personal identification number (PIN) and ownership percentage. Ownership has to add up to 100%. You can add as many owners as your business type allows.
Registered agentThe person who receives legal papers for the business: full legal name, date of birth and PIN. The agent can be an owner.
CertificationYou certify that the information is accurate, that the business is authorized to operate under applicable law, that you are authorized to submit the registration, and that you will notify the Department of State of material changes.
Electronic signatureYou sign by typing your full legal name. The signature is dated the day you submit.
Every owner and the registered agent are checked against their vital record. The PIN and date of birth have to match. You have to be listed as an owner or the registered agent.

Industries

You choose one industry: agriculture and farming, automotive sales and repair, construction and contracting, education and training, entertainment and media, finance and insurance, food and beverage, government contracting, health and wellness, hospitality and lodging, landscaping and outdoor services, legal and professional services, manufacturing, nonprofit and community services, personal services, real estate and property management, retail, security services, technology and software, transportation and logistics, utilities and energy, or other.

Name rules

TypeThe name must include
Limited liability company"LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
Corporation"Inc.", "Corp.", "Company" or "Limited"
Nonprofit corporationNothing required, but it cannot suggest it is a for-profit company
Limited partnership"LP" or "Limited Partnership"
Sole proprietorship, general partnershipNothing required

A name can be refused if it is already in use by an active business of the same type, if it suggests a government agency (for example "Michigan State Police Supply"), or if it suggests a licensed profession the owners do not hold.

How to register

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Business ServicesChoose Register a business.
  3. Fill in the registrationBusiness information, address, owners, and the registered agent.
  4. Certify and signTick the four certifications, type your full legal name as your electronic signature, and submit.
  5. Wait for reviewThe Division approves or denies the application. You can follow it in e-Services.

After you are approved

  • Your business is Active and on the public record with its Business ID, a nine digit number written like 47-1938265.
  • Your approval date and, where an annual report is required, your expiration date are recorded.
  • The filing fee is charged to your SOS account.
  • If your business type needs one, your first annual report is due on the anniversary of your approval. See annual reports.
An application with false information can be denied, and a business registered on false information can be administratively dissolved.

Questions

Can I change my business name later? Yes. File an amendment in Business Services. The same name rules apply.

Do I need a separate license? Only if the business does regulated work. Medical and bail bond work need a professional license.

Can someone else file for me? No. You file from your own account, and your PIN is recorded as the filer.
