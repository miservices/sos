Annual reports

An annual report confirms that your business is still operating and that the information on the public record is correct. Limited liability companies, corporations, nonprofit corporations and limited partnerships have to file one every year. Sole proprietorships and general partnerships do not.

When it is due

Your report is due on the anniversary of the day your business was approved. You can file it up to 60 days early. Your due date is shown on your business in Business Services.

The report fee is charged when the Division accepts your report.

What you report

  • Whether the business is still operating.
  • The current business name and what it does.
  • The registered agent.
  • Every current owner, member, partner or officer, by PIN. Add anyone new and remove anyone who has left.

If you miss the due date

WhenWhat happens
On the due dateThe business is still Active.
Up to 30 days lateThe business is Past due. File the report and pay the report fee plus the late fee to return to active.
More than 30 days lateThe business is administratively dissolved. Its good standing is lost and its name can be registered by someone else.

How to file

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse the account of an owner or officer of the business.
  2. Open Business ServicesChoose your business, then File annual report.
  3. Review the detailsYour current information is filled in. Correct anything that has changed.
  4. Confirm and submitTick the box to confirm the information is true.
  5. Wait for reviewThe Division accepts the report and your due date moves forward one year.
Dissolved by mistake? A business that was administratively dissolved can apply to be reinstated in Business Services by filing the missing reports and paying the fees owed.

Questions

Do I have to file if the business made no money? Yes. The report is about the business being active, not its income.

I closed my business. Do I still file? No. Dissolve it in Business Services instead, so it is not marked past due.

Who can file? An owner, member, partner or officer of the business.
