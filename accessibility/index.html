Our commitment

The Department of State wants every Michigander to be able to use its services. We aim to make this website usable by people with a wide range of abilities, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, or magnification.

What we do

  • Use headings, lists, and landmarks so screen readers can move through each page.
  • Make every page usable with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline.
  • Keep text readable with strong color contrast and text that resizes with your browser.
  • Write in plain language and avoid relying on color alone to carry meaning.
  • Make pages work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Tips for using this site

  1. Zoom the pageUse your browser's zoom (Ctrl or Command with plus) to enlarge text. Layouts adjust to fit.
  2. Use the keyboardPress Tab to move between links and buttons, and Enter to select. The main menu opens when it has focus.
  3. Use a screen readerNavigate by headings to jump between sections of a page.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, tell us what page you were on and what happened. Include the assistive technology you use, if any. Find the right contact in the office directory.

This website is a simulated government interface for a fictional Michigan. It does not represent any real government body or authority.
