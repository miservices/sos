What the Department does

The Michigan Department of State is led by the Secretary of State. The Department licenses drivers, keeps the state's vital records, runs elections, authenticates official documents, and hears appeals of government decisions. Most services are available online through SOS e-Services with a MiGOVT account.

How the Department is organized

Three bureaus carry out the Department's work.

Licensing and Regulatory AffairsLicenses and credentials people, records, and professions through the Divisions of Driver Licensing and Records, Vital Records and Citizenship, and Professional Licensing.
ElectionsRuns state and local elections, voter registration, campaign finance reporting, and compliance with election law. The Office of the Great Seal is part of this bureau.
Administrative Hearings and AppealsHolds impartial hearings and decides appeals. It is led by the Chief Administrative Law Judge.

Divisions and offices

  • Division of Driver Licensing and Records Driver's, motorcycle, chauffeur, and commercial licenses, personal ID cards, suspensions, and driving records.
  • Division of Vital Records and Citizenship Birth certificates, citizenship records, and name changes.
  • Division of Business and Occupational Licensing Business registration, annual reports, and the entity registry, plus licenses for medical and other regulated professionals.
  • Elections State and local elections, voter registration, campaign finance reporting, and election law compliance.
  • Office of the Great Seal Official repository of public records, notary commissions, and authentication of official documents.
  • Administrative Law Judges Impartial hearings and appeals of decisions by state agencies, counties, and municipalities.
