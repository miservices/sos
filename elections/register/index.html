Voter registration

Michigan citizens and residents are registered to vote automatically.

Check status

You do not need to register

If you are a Michigan citizen or a resident, you are registered to vote automatically. Your registration comes from your vital record, so there is no form to file and nothing to renew.

Your statusRegistered to vote?
CitizenYes, automatically
ResidentYes, automatically
No status on your vital recordNo

Confirm your registration

  1. Check your registration statusEnter your PIN and your date of birth.
  2. See your status on your recordYou can also view it under Identification in SOS e-Services.
  3. VoteVoting is open to everyone who is registered. See absentee voting and polling places.

If your status is missing

If your vital record does not show citizen or resident, you are not registered. Your status is kept on your vital record. Contact the Bureau of Elections through the office directory if you believe your status is wrong.

Candidates must also be citizens or residents. Candidate filing is done in Candidate Services.
