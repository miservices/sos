Who can run

You can file to run for office if you are a Michigan citizen or a resident. Citizens and residents are registered to vote automatically, and that same status makes you eligible to file.

How to file

  1. Sign in to Candidate ServicesUse your MiGOVT account in SOS e-Services.
  2. Choose your officePick the state, county, or city office you are running for. Candidates for Governor also name a Lieutenant Governor running mate, since the two are elected as one ticket.
  3. Add a statementYou can add a short candidate statement. It is optional.
  4. Submit your filingThe Bureau of Elections reviews it. When it is approved you are certified as a candidate and listed on this page.
  5. Report your campaign financesSubmit finance reports in Candidate Services. Approved reports are published on the campaign finance page.
