How results are decided

Single seat races go to the candidate with the most votes. Ranked preference races go to the candidates with the highest total preference scores. A tie is settled by a runoff, except for a tie for Speaker, Chief Justice, Chairperson, or President of the Council, which is broken by the newly elected Governor or Mayor. Read how elections work.
