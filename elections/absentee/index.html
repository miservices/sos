How absentee voting works

You do not have to be at a polling place to vote. Every general election has an online voting window that opens 30 days before the term begins and closes 10 days before it. Any registered voter can vote online during that window.

How to vote online

  1. Make sure you are registeredCitizens and residents are registered automatically.
  2. Open Voting ServicesSign in to SOS e-Services with your MiGOVT account while the window is open.
  3. Mark your ballotVote in each state race. For ranked preference races, rank up to the number of open seats.
  4. SubmitReview your ballot and submit it before the window closes.

State, county and city elections

Online voting for the state election runs through the Secretary of State's online system. County and city elections use a Secretary of State approved system, so your ballot may appear in more than one place.

Runoffs and special elections

A runoff has its own online window, January 1 to January 10 of the term year, and an in person Election Day on January 5. A special election has the same two part structure on a timeline set by the Secretary of State for state offices, or by the County Clerk for county and city offices.

Voting in person on Election Day is also available. See polling places.
