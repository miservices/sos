Vehicle registration

Every vehicle you drive on Michigan roads has to be registered. Register online in SOS e-Services or in person at a branch office.

Register onlineFind a branch

You must register your vehicle

A vehicle must be registered with the Secretary of State before it is driven. You can register it two ways, and the result is the same.

Online, in SOS e-ServicesSign in with your MiGOVT account, add the vehicle, and pay the fee. Registration is recorded on your account.Register online In person, at a branch officeVisit any Secretary of State branch. A clerk registers the vehicle for you and takes your payment.Find a branch

How to register online

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesYou need a MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Vehicle RegistrationYou can see every vehicle already registered to you.
  3. Add your vehicleEnter the vehicle's details, such as its plate and description.
  4. Pay the registration feeThe fee is charged to your account and recorded in your Transactions and History.
  5. Your vehicle is registeredYou can see the registration, and when it expires, in e-Services.

How to register at a branch

  1. Visit a branch officeFind one in the office directory.
  2. Tell the clerk about the vehicleGive your name, your PIN, and the vehicle's details.
  3. Pay the feeThe clerk registers the vehicle on your account. It then shows up in e-Services like any other registration.
Insurance and title. Michigan vehicles also need auto insurance. If you bought the vehicle from someone else, you may need a title transfer.

After you register

Registrations expire and must be renewed. See registration renewal for how, and what happens if you let one lapse.
