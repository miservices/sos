Plate designs

These are the license plate designs issued by the Secretary of State. Each is shown with the word SAMPLE where the plate number goes.

Blue

A deep blue face with a darker blue band.

Blackout

Pure black, tone on tone. Only the characters stand out.

Standard

The everyday plate. A white face with a dark blue band.

Emergency service

For police, fire, and EMS vehicles. The standard plate with a gold star, gold trim, and a gold edged tab.

Emergency service plates are for police, fire, and EMS vehicles. Every other design is available for personal vehicles.

Get a plate

A plate is issued when you register your vehicle, online in SOS e-Services or in person at a branch. Keep it current with registration renewal.
