Auto insurance

Michigan vehicles need insurance

Every vehicle on Michigan roads needs auto insurance. Your insurance is tied to your vehicle registration, and you should keep it in place for as long as the vehicle is registered.

Insurance is provided by licensed insurers

Auto insurance in Michigan is sold by insurance companies that are licensed by the Secretary of State. The Department regulates these insurers, so you can buy a policy knowing the company is licensed.

See it in e-Services. Insurance Services shows your coverage status, your proof of insurance, whether you are in compliance, and a list of licensed insurers.

What to do

  1. Choose a licensed insurerFind one in the list of insurers in Insurance Services, and arrange coverage for your vehicle.
  2. Register your vehicleHave your insurance in place when you register the vehicle.
  3. The insurer registers itself on your vehicleYour insurer records itself as the insurer of your vehicle. Open Insurance Services to confirm your coverage status and get your proof of insurance.
  4. Keep it currentIf your policy ends, get a new one before you drive. Renew your registration on time with registration renewal.

If you sell or replace a vehicle

When you sell a vehicle, transfer the title. A new vehicle needs its own registration and its own insurance. Dealerships handle the title for you when you buy from them, but you still need to insure the vehicle.

For insurance companies

Insurance companies use Insurance Provider Services in SOS e-Services to apply for their auto insurance license and to register themselves as the insurer of a vehicle. It appears in the e-Services menu once your account has been approved as a licensed insurer.

Driving without insurance can lead to a citation.
