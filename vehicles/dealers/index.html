Dealerships in Michigan

Car dealers in Michigan are licensed by the Secretary of State. A licensed dealership can sell vehicles and handle the paperwork that goes with a sale, so buyers do not have to.

If you are buying from a dealership

The dealership handles your title automatically. Every in-state dealership applies for the title for you when you buy from them. You do not need to file a title application or a transfer.

Dealers also work with the Department on registration for buyers. Your vehicle's registration and title show up on your account, and you can check them in Vehicle Registration and Vehicle Titles in SOS e-Services. Michigan vehicles also need auto insurance.

If you are selling or buying privately

The dealership rule only applies to dealerships. If you buy from or sell to a person, you handle the title yourself. See title transfer.

If you run a dealership

Licensed dealers use Dealer Services in SOS e-Services. It is the place to manage your dealership license and to file title and registration applications for your buyers, vehicle sales, and ownership transfers. Dealer Services appears in your e-Services menu once your account has been approved as a licensed dealer.

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Dealer ServicesFind it under Industry Services.
  3. Apply for your dealership licenseSubmit your dealership license application.
  4. Handle your salesWhen you sell a vehicle, file the title and registration for the buyer.
Not licensed yet? Contact the Secretary of State through the office directory to get your account approved as a dealer.
