Renew before it expires

A vehicle registration does not last forever. Renew it before the expiration date so your vehicle stays registered. You can see every registration you hold, and when each one expires, in SOS e-Services.

Online, in SOS e-ServicesOpen Vehicle Registration, pick the vehicle, and renew it. The fee is charged to your account.Renew online In person, at a branch officeVisit a Secretary of State branch and a clerk renews it for you.Find a branch

How to renew online

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Vehicle RegistrationFind the vehicle you want to renew.
  3. Renew itConfirm the renewal and pay the fee.
  4. Check the new expiration dateThe renewal is saved on your account and recorded in your Transactions and History.

If your registration has expired

An expired registration means the vehicle is no longer registered. Renew it as soon as you can. If a registration has been expired for a long time, you may need to register the vehicle again.

Driving with an expired registration can lead to a citation.

Selling or moving a vehicle

If you sold the vehicle, handle the title transfer. If you have a new vehicle, register it.
