What a title is

A title is the official record that you own a vehicle. The Secretary of State keeps it, and it names the owner of the vehicle. You apply for a title when you own a vehicle that does not have one in your name. You can apply two ways, and the result is the same.

Online, in SOS e-ServicesSign in with your MiGOVT account, apply for the title, and pay the fee. How to apply online

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesYou need a MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Vehicle TitlesYou can see every vehicle you are already titled in.
  3. Start a title applicationEnter the vehicle's details, such as its plate and description.
  4. Pay the title feeThe fee is charged to your account and recorded in your Transactions and History.
  5. Your application is reviewedThe Department of State reviews it. When it is approved you are titled in the vehicle, and you can see it in e-Services.

How to apply at a branch

  1. Visit a branch officeFind one in the office directory.
  2. Tell the clerk about the vehicleGive your name, your PIN, and the vehicle's details.
  3. Pay the feeThe clerk records the title on your account. It then shows up in e-Services like any other title.

Title and registration are different. A title shows who owns a vehicle. A registration lets you drive it on Michigan roads, and it has to be renewed. Michigan vehicles also need auto insurance.
