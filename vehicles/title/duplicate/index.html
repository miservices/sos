When you need a duplicate title

A duplicate title is a replacement copy of your vehicle's title. Get one if your title was lost, stolen, or damaged. Only the person the title is issued to can ask for a duplicate.

Buying from a dealership. Every in-state dealership handles titles automatically. If you need a replacement later, you can request it here.

Where to get a duplicate

How to request a duplicate online

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesYou need a MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Vehicle TitlesYou can see every vehicle you are titled in.
  3. Choose the vehiclePick the vehicle whose title you need replaced and request a duplicate.
  4. Pay the feeThe fee is charged to your account and recorded in your Transactions and History.
  5. Your duplicate is issuedThe Department of State reviews the request. When it is approved the duplicate is recorded on your account.

How to request a duplicate at a branch

  1. Visit a branch officeFind one in the office directory.
  2. Tell the clerk which vehicleGive your name, your PIN, and the vehicle's details.
  3. Pay the feeThe clerk issues the duplicate and records it on your account.

Good to know

  • A duplicate replaces the original. You are still the owner. Nothing about who owns the vehicle changes.
  • If you are selling the vehicle and the title is lost, get a duplicate first, then do the title transfer.
  • You do not need a title to renew a registration.
If you are not titled in the vehicle yet, you need a title application, not a duplicate.
