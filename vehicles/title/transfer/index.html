Title transfer

Move a vehicle's title to a new owner when you sell it or buy it from someone who is not a dealership.

When you need a title transfer

A title transfer moves ownership of a vehicle from one person to another. You need one when a vehicle is sold or given to someone who is not a dealership.

Buying from a dealership. Every in-state dealership handles the title for you automatically. You do not need to transfer anything. See car dealers.

Where to transfer a title

Online, in SOS e-ServicesSign in with your MiGOVT account and transfer the title from your Vehicle Titles page.Transfer online In person, at a branch officeVisit any Secretary of State branch. A clerk records the transfer for you.Find a branch

If you are selling

  1. Agree on the saleKnow the buyer's name and PIN.
  2. Transfer the title to the buyerStart the transfer in Vehicle Titles, or ask a clerk at a branch.
  3. Confirm it went throughThe vehicle should no longer appear under your titles.

If you are buying

  1. Agree on the saleKnow the seller's name and PIN.
  2. Take the titleThe seller transfers it to you, or you complete the transfer at a branch.
  3. Check your titlesThe vehicle should appear under Vehicle Titles in SOS e-Services.
  4. Register the vehicleA transfer changes who owns the vehicle. To drive it you must have it registered, and you need auto insurance.

Fees

The transfer fee is charged to your account and recorded in your Transactions and History. At a branch, the clerk takes your payment.

Lost or damaged title? Get a replacement first. See duplicate titles.
