Handicap parking in Michigan

Handicap parking spaces are reserved for people with a disability that affects how they get around. To park in one, a vehicle has to display a handicap parking placard or carry a handicap plate issued by the Secretary of State.

How to apply

  1. Choose a placard or a plateA placard goes with you. A plate goes with a vehicle.
  2. Apply online or at a branchUse SOS e-Services, or visit a branch.
  3. The Department reviews your applicationThe Department of State decides whether you qualify.
  4. Receive your placard or plateOnce approved it is recorded on your account.

Using handicap parking

  • The placard or plate must belong to the person who uses it. Using someone else's to park in a reserved space is not allowed.
  • Keep it current. Placards and plates can expire and must be renewed.
  • A handicap plate is still a plate on a registered vehicle, so keep the registration current with registration renewal.
Parking in a handicap space without a placard or plate can lead to a citation.
