Search vital records

Look up a person by PIN, name, date of birth, or city of birth. Fill in any of the boxes. Everything you enter has to match. You can then open a result to see their details, including their name change history.

People are told when they are looked up. Two different entries appear in a person's history: one when they show up in your search results, and a separate one when you open their result. Both show your name.

Other lookups

To look up a person's license, driving record, vehicle registration, title, or insurance, approved accounts can use Records Access in SOS e-Services. To see your own record, use Update my information.
