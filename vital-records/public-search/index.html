Public record search

How looking up another person's records works.

Records Access

What you can look up

Approved accounts can look up another person's license status, driving record, vehicle registration, title, or insurance. This is called records access.

RecordWhere it comes from
License statusLicenses & IDs
Driving recordDriving records
Vehicle registrationVehicle registration
TitleTitle applications
InsuranceAuto insurance

Who can use it

Records access is given to approved accounts. When your account has it, Records Access appears in your SOS e-Services menu. If you do not see it, your account has not been approved.

How looking someone up works

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Records AccessIt appears only for approved accounts.
  3. Search by PINChoose the person and the record you need.
Lookups are recorded. Every time someone looks up your records, it is logged. You can see who has looked at your license in Driver Licensing.
