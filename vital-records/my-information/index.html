Your MiGOVT account

Your MiGOVT account pulls your name, PIN, and date of birth from your vital record. MiGOVT reads the data, but only the Department can change it. When a name change is approved, your MiGOVT account name is updated too.

Your names

Your record keeps every name you have had, with the dates you used them. You can see your name at birth and your former names in Identification.

How to update your information

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open IdentificationYour information is shown at the top.
  3. Apply for a name changeNames are the only thing you can change yourself. It costs $650.00 for one name and $500.00 for each additional one.
Date of birth is permanent. Names can be changed online for a fee. Date of birth can never be changed.
