The Certificate of Live Birth

Your Certificate of Live Birth is the official record of your birth. You can view and print it from Identification in SOS e-Services, once a birth record is on file for you.

What it shows

DetailNotes
Your name at birthThe name recorded when you were born, even if you have changed your name since
Date of birthPermanent. It can never be changed
Time of birthShown if it was recorded
City and county of birthShown if they were recorded
Certificate numberA number unique to your certificate

How to get yours

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open IdentificationFind the Certificate of Live Birth.
  3. View itThe certificate is built from your vital record.
  4. Print itPrint or save the certificate.

If it says no birth record

If no birth record is on file for you, there is nothing to print yet. Contact the Department through the office directory.

Changed your name? Your birth certificate keeps your name at birth. Your later names are kept as previous names on your record. See name changes.
