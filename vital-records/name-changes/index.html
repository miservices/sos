Changing your name

You can apply to change your name for any reason. Name changes are done online in Identification in SOS e-Services. The Department reviews every application.

Fees

Names you changeFee
One name (first, middle, or last)$650.00
Each additional name$500.00
Example: first and last name$650.00 plus $500.00 = $1,150.00

The fee is charged only if your application is approved.

The rules

  • You can have only one open application at a time.
  • You can apply once every 90 days.
  • Your date of birth can never be changed.

How to apply

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open IdentificationGo to Apply for a name change.
  3. Edit your nameYour current name is filled in. Change the first, middle, or last name. The fee updates as you type.
  4. Confirm and submitTick the box to confirm you understand the fee and that your date of birth cannot be changed.
  5. Wait for reviewThe Department reviews your application. You can follow it in e-Services.

When it is approved

  • Your new name is recorded on your vital record, and your previous names are kept with the dates you used them.
  • Your MiGOVT account name is updated.
  • Your name is updated in court records that match your PIN, with your former name kept as an alias.
  • Your Secretary of State account is updated and the fee is charged.
Your birth certificate always shows the name you were born with.
