Citizenship

Your citizenship status is kept on your vital record. It decides what you can do.

See your status

The three statuses

StatusWhat it means
CitizenYou are a citizen of Michigan.
ResidentYou live in Michigan but are not a citizen.
NoneYour vital record shows neither status.

The Department of State records your status on your vital record. You can see it in Identification in SOS e-Services.

Certificate of citizenship or residency

If your status is Citizen or Resident you can print a certificate from Identification. A citizen gets a Certificate of Citizenship, and a resident gets a Certificate of Residency. If your status is None, there is no certificate to print.

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open IdentificationFind the certificate of citizenship or residency.
  3. Print itThe certificate shows your status, date of birth, place of birth and file number.

If your status is wrong

Only the Department can change your status. Contact the Department through the office directory.
