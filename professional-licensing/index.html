Professional licensing

The Department of State licenses physicians, paramedics, advanced EMTs, EMTs, and bail bond agents.

Licenses we issue

The Department of State licenses medical professionals and bail bond agents. Each license is held by one person and has its own number.

Medical license: PhysicianPractice medicine as a physician.Medical
Medical license: ParamedicProvide emergency medical care as a paramedic.Medical
Medical license: Advanced EMTProvide emergency care as an advanced emergency medical technician.Medical
Medical license: EMTProvide emergency care as an emergency medical technician.Medical
Bail bond agent licenseWrite bail bonds as a licensed bail bond agent.Bail bonds
Attorneys are licensed by the courts. Legal licensing is not handled here. See the Michigan Courts.

License statuses

StatusWhat it means
ActiveIn good standing. The holder may practice.
InactiveHeld, but not in use. The holder may not practice until it is reactivated.
ProbationaryThe holder may practice while meeting the terms of probation.
ConditionalThe holder may practice only within the listed conditions.
SuspendedTemporarily not allowed to practice.
RevokedPermanently removed. The holder may not practice.
ExpiredPast its expiration date. It must be renewed before the holder can practice again.

How applications and renewals work

You do not apply for a professional license yourself. An application or a renewal is filed on your behalf by an endorsing official, or by the Department of State, and the Department reviews it. When it is approved your license is issued and shows on your account.

  1. An endorsing official files for youOfficials who are approved to endorse a license type file the application or renewal.
  2. The Department reviews itThe Department of State approves or denies the request.
  3. Your license appearsAn approved license is recorded with its number, issue date, and expiration date.
  4. Renew before it expiresLicenses last 1 year. Ask your endorsing official or the Department to renew it.
