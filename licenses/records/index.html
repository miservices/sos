Driving Records

Your lifetime record, who can see it, and how to view or request a certified copy.

Open Driving Record

What your driving record shows

Your driving record is the official, lifetime history of your driving privileges. It does not reset.

  • Your license classification, standard or restricted
  • Any endorsements or licenses you hold, such as motorcycle or chauffeur's
  • All points ever added to your record, including points that have expired
  • Any warning letters, suspensions, and revocations
  • Your testing history

Reading your points

Points count toward warning letters and suspensions only during a 30 day period, and thresholds differ for standard and restricted licenses. Once the 30 days pass, the points expire, but they stay on your lifetime record. See the point system.

Only the highest-point violation from a single incident is counted. If your record shows more than that, you can contest the points.

Who can see your record

Your record is private. Only these can access it without your permission:

  • You, the driver
  • Government and law enforcement agencies
  • Insurance companies
  • Anyone with a subpoena

Everyone else, including employers and any other person or business, needs your approval in the system first. Requests appear in Access Approvals, where you can approve or deny them.

See who has accessed your record

You are entitled to see when someone accessed your record. The access history is in Driving Record in SOS e-Services. It shows who looked, and when. The one exception is searches made through the law enforcement information network, the database officers commonly check during a traffic stop. Those searches do not appear in your history.

How to get your record

Records are not issued on this page. You can view your record and request a certified copy in SOS e-Services.

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account.
  2. Open Driving RecordYour record is shown as soon as you open it.
  3. Request a certified copyUse the request button on the same page when you need an official copy.
