What your driving record shows

Your driving record is the official history of your driving privileges.

  • Your license classification, standard or restricted
  • Any endorsements or licenses you hold, such as motorcycle or chauffeur's
  • Points on your record in the current 30 day period
  • Any warning letters, suspensions, and revocations
  • Your testing history

Reading your points

Points are counted over a 30 day period, and thresholds differ for standard and restricted licenses. See the point system.

Only the highest-point violation from a single incident is counted. If your record shows more than that, you can contest the points.

How to get your record

Records are not issued on this page. To get your driving record, go to SOS e-Services and sign in with your MiGOVT account.

  1. Open SOS e-ServicesSign in with your MiGOVT account.
  2. Choose Driving RecordsSelect the driving record service from the portal.
  3. View or downloadYour record is available as soon as you request it.
