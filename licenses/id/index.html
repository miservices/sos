Personal ID & REAL ID

A state ID if you do not hold a driver's license, and the REAL ID for entering military buildings.

Find a branchIdentification

Personal identification card

If you do not have a driver's license, you are issued a personal identification (PI) card in its place. A driver's license is always issued in preference to a PI card whenever you qualify for one.

First-time applicants

Physical cards are issued in person. Go to a Secretary of State branch to get your card. Find one in the office directory.
  1. Visit a branchBring what you need to prove who you are. Your name, PIN, and date of birth are already on your MiGOVT account.
  2. Pay the feeA PI card carries an additional $15.00 fee.
  3. Receive your cardThe branch issues your physical card.

Renewing

Renew your personal ID card in person at a Secretary of State branch.

REAL ID

A REAL ID is a card that allows you to enter military buildings. To get a REAL ID you must be a citizen and pay the additional fee. Like all physical cards, it is issued in person at a branch. Your citizenship status is shown in Identification.

  • You must be a citizen.
  • The REAL ID carries an additional $15.00 fee.
  • A REAL ID lets you enter military buildings.
