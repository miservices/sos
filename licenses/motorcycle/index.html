Motorcycle Endorsement

Add motorcycle privileges to your standard license by passing the online test.

Take the test nowOpen SOS e-Services

What the endorsement is

A motorcycle license is an endorsement added to your standard driver's license. It follows the same path as the driver's license, but once your waiting period is over it becomes a motorcycle endorsement on your existing license.

Requirements

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must be a citizen and/or a resident. Someone who is neither cannot apply.
  • You must hold a standard license. A restricted license is not eligible.
  • Your license must not be suspended or revoked.
Your statusWhat happens after you pass
CitizenA restricted motorcycle endorsement is issued in the system. After 10 days, as long as your license is not suspended or revoked, it becomes a full endorsement.
ResidentYou automatically receive the full motorcycle endorsement.

How to get the endorsement

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesMake sure your standard license is active.
  2. Take the online motorcycle testThe application fee is $135.00.
  3. Score above 70%A passing score is above 70 percent.
  4. Endorsement issued in the systemA card is not issued automatically. Request an updated card in e-Services when you want one.
  5. Waiting periodCitizens complete 10 days before the endorsement is fully active. Residents skip the wait.

If your license is suspended or revoked

You lose your motorcycle endorsement. Getting your license back does not give the endorsement back. You must retest and pay the application fee again.

The retesting rules for the restricted period are the same as for the driver's license: wait at least 72 hours after a suspension or revocation ends before you retest, and no more than 8 restricted licenses may be issued. See Driver's licenses and Suspensions & revocations.
