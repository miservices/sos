Standard and restricted licenses

Every driver's license is one of two classifications. A restricted license is where new drivers start. A standard license is the full license, and it is required before you can add a motorcycle endorsement, a chauffeur's license, or a commercial license. Restricted drivers also face lower point limits. Read how the driver's license works.
