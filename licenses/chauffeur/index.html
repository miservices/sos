What a chauffeur's license is

A chauffeur's license allows you to drive passengers for hire. It is more demanding than a regular driver's license, and it is a separate license from a commercial driver's license (CDL), which is for large and heavy vehicles. Compare it with a CDL.

Requirements

  • You must be a citizen.
  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must hold a standard license. A restricted license is not eligible.
  • Your license must not be suspended or revoked.

How to get your chauffeur's license

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesConfirm your standard license is active.
  2. Take the online chauffeur's testThe test fee is $175.00.
  3. Score above 70%A passing score is above 70 percent.
  4. License issuedYour chauffeur's license is added to your record. Request an updated card in e-Services when you want one.

If your license is suspended or revoked

You lose your chauffeur's license. Getting your driver's license back does not give it back. You must retest and pay the test fee again.
