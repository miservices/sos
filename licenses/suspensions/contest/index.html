Contest Points

Ask the Department to review points added to your record.

If you believe points were added to your record by mistake, or that an officer misused the system, submit this form. If your contest is substantiated, all points from that entire incident are voided, including the highest one.

Only the highest-point violation from a single stop or incident is counted. If more than one violation from the same incident was added to your record, that is grounds to contest.
