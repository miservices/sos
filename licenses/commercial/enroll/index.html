About CDL training

CDL training is provided directly by the Secretary of State. Training from private instructors is not accepted toward a CDL.

To enroll when it opens

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must hold a standard driver's license, not a restricted one.
  • You will be assessed dues during registration, which must be paid before you can test.

After training, you will take a knowledge test and then a skills test. See Commercial driver's license for the full process.
