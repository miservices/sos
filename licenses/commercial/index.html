What a CDL covers

A commercial driver's license (CDL) is for operating large or heavy vehicles. Unlike the chauffeur's license, which you earn by passing an online test, a CDL requires training provided directly by the Secretary of State, followed by a knowledge test and a skills test.

License classes

ClassCovers
Class ACombination vehicles with a combined weight of 26,001 pounds or more where the towed unit is over 10,000 pounds, such as tractor-trailers.
Class BSingle vehicles of 26,001 pounds or more, such as straight trucks, dump trucks, and large buses.
Class CVehicles that do not fit Class A or B but carry 16 or more passengers, or carry hazardous materials that require placards.

Requirements

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must hold a standard license. A restricted license is not eligible.
  • You must complete CDL training provided directly by the Secretary of State. Private instructors are not accepted.

How to get your CDL

  1. Enroll in SOS CDL trainingSubmit your enrollment at the enrollment page.
  2. Pay your assessed duesYour dues are provided during registration and must be paid to continue.
  3. Pass the knowledge testPay the test fee, then take the knowledge test.
  4. Pass the skills testThe skills test follows the knowledge test.
  5. Pay the license feeOnce you pass the skills test and the license fee is paid, your CDL is issued.
