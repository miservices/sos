Driver's Licenses

Pass the online test to be issued your license. Citizens advance to a standard license after 10 days, and residents receive it immediately.

Who can get a license

The only requirement to become a licensed driver is that you are at least 18 years old. Being a citizen makes the process easier, and being a resident makes it the easiest. Your status decides how far you can advance after you pass the test.

Your statusWhat happens after you pass
CitizenYou are issued a restricted license. After you have held it for 10 days you are advanced to a standard license automatically, as long as the license is not suspended or revoked.
ResidentYou automatically receive your full standard license.
Not a citizen or a residentYou are issued a restricted license, but you cannot advance to a standard license.

How to get your license

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesYou need a MiGOVT account. You must be 18 or older.
  2. Take the online driver's testThe test fee is $75.00. You can take it online, any time.
  3. Score above 70%A passing score is above 70 percent. A score of exactly 70 percent does not pass.
  4. Your license is issued in the systemA restricted license is issued automatically when you pass. A physical card is not issued automatically.
  5. Advance to standardResidents receive the full license immediately. Citizens are advanced after 10 days with a restricted license that is not suspended or revoked.
  6. Request your cardWhen you want the physical card, request it in Driver Licensing. The card fee is $55.00.
If you are not a citizen or a resident, you cannot advance past the restricted license.

If your restricted license is suspended or revoked

If you receive a suspension or revocation while you hold a restricted license, you must wait at least 72 hours after it ends before you can retest. A new test fee applies each time you retest.

Think a point total or suspension is wrong? You can contest the points from an incident.

Renewing your license

Your driver's license must be renewed every 12 months. Renew it in Driver Licensing in SOS e-Services. You do not need to retake the test to renew.

Every renewal is recorded in your account history, which you can review under Transactions and History.
