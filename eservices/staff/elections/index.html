Elections Desk

Voter registration, candidates, campaign finance, and the Office of the Great Seal. Part of the Bureau of Elections.

Election calendar

Every general election runs on this calendar: one year terms, Election Day 21 days before the term begins, and online voting from 30 to 10 days before it.

What this desk handles

  • Voters. Citizens and residents are registered automatically from their vital record, so there is nothing to approve.
  • Candidate filings. Approving one certifies the candidate for that office and lists them on Candidate Services.
  • Campaign finance reports
  • Business registrations (Office of the Great Seal)
  • Notary commissions (Office of the Great Seal)
