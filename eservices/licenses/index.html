Identity and Licensing

Driver Licensing

Your driver's license, endorsements and commercial license in one place: status, renewal every 12 months, cards, and who has looked at your license.

Your licenses

Renew

Every license must be renewed every 12 months. You do not retake the test. Renewal opens 60 days before a license expires, and an expired license can be renewed any time. Each renewal is reviewed by the Department and recorded in your Transactions and History.

Cards

Your license is active in the system as soon as it is issued. A physical card is not issued automatically. Request one when you want it. The card fee is $55.00, charged when the request is approved.
