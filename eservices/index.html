SOS e-Services

Your online portal for Secretary of State services. Sign in with your MiGOVT account to get started.

Login with MiGOVT

How sign-in works

  1. Choose Login with MiGOVTYou are sent to MiGOVT Login, the single sign-in used across Michigan services.
  2. Sign inUse Roblox, Discord, Google, or your email and password.
  3. Your account is created for youThe first time you sign in, SOS e-Services creates your account automatically. There is nothing else to fill out.

What you can do in e-Services

  • Take your driver's, motorcycle, and chauffeur's tests online
  • Contest points added to your driving record
  • Manage licenses, vehicles, vital records, and business filings as these services open
Your name, PIN, and date of birth come from your MiGOVT account, which pulls them from the Department of State's vital records.
