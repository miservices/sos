What Happens Next

  • Your application is deemed received on the next calendar day following submission.
  • The Bureau will take action within 10 days of receipt — approval, denial, or a conditional exception.
  • If a conditional exception is issued, you will have 2–4 days to correct or clarify the identified issue.
  • Total processing time will not exceed 15 calendar days from the date of receipt.
  • All notices will be delivered through this portal and will be accessible in your application record.
