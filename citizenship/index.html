Michigan State Citizenship

Learn about Michigan citizenship — what it means, who qualifies, how to apply, and how your identity is recorded in the Michigan Identity Registry.

What is Michigan Citizenship?

Michigan citizenship is a formal legal status granted to residents who register with the Michigan Identity Registry and are recognized by the state as a citizen under the Citizenship and National Status Act (Public Act 13).

A citizen is a person who has been lawfully recognized as a citizen of the state pursuant to established procedures, and whose identity and citizenship status have been recorded in the Michigan Identity Registry. Once granted, citizenship entitles you to all rights and privileges provided by state law.

🪪

Citizen

A person lawfully recognized as a citizen with their identity recorded in the Michigan Identity Registry.

🌐

National

A person with a recognized legal affiliation with the state who may or may not hold full citizenship status.

🔗

Dual Citizenship

Michigan recognizes dual citizenship — holding status in another jurisdiction does not affect your eligibility here.

📋

Michigan Identity Registry

The official state registry that records your identity, citizenship status, and links your roleplay identity to your Roblox account.

How to Apply for Citizenship

Citizenship is obtained by submitting a self-registration to the Michigan Identity Registry that includes a petition for citizenship. The Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records reviews all applications within 30 days.

1

Submit a Self-Registration

Complete the online self-registration form through the Michigan Identity Registry. Provide accurate and complete identifying information — your name, date of birth, Social Security Number, and Roblox account details.

2

Check the Citizenship Petition Box

During registration, you must affirmatively check the box indicating your intent to seek recognition as a citizen of the state. This designates your registration as a Petition for Citizenship.

3

Registrar Review

The Registrar of the Michigan Identity Registry will review your petition within 30 days to determine whether you satisfy all requirements established under law and that no grounds for denial exist.

4

Citizenship Granted & Recorded

If approved, citizenship is granted and your status is officially recorded in the Michigan Identity Registry. You are then recognized as a citizen of the State of Michigan for all purposes provided by law.

Ready to apply? The online citizenship application is available 24/7 through the Michigan Identity Registry portal.

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for citizenship under the Citizenship and National Status Act, all of the following must be satisfied:

✅
Complete Self-Registration
Submit a self-registration to the Michigan Identity Registry with accurate and complete identifying information including your first and last name, date of birth, and Social Security Number.
✅
Affirm Intent for Citizenship
Actively indicate your intent to seek recognition as a citizen by checking the designated checkbox on the registration form.
✅
Roblox Account Link
Provide your Roblox platform identifier (account ID) so your roleplay identity — name and date of birth — can be linked in the Michigan Identity Registry.
✅
No Grounds for Denial
You must not be listed in the Restricted Persons Database, have a court order prohibiting citizenship, or have been convicted of treason against the State of Michigan.

Dual Citizenship: Holding citizenship, nationality, or legal status in another jurisdiction does not affect your eligibility. Michigan law explicitly prohibits using dual citizenship as a negative factor in reviewing a petition.

Grounds for Denial or Revocation

Citizenship will be denied or, if already granted, revoked under the following circumstances:

🚫
Restricted Persons Database
Placement in the Restricted Persons Database — a registry of individuals determined by a court to pose a significant threat to the state or convicted of treason.
🚫
Conviction of Treason
A conviction of treason against the State of Michigan is an absolute bar to citizenship.
🚫
Court Order
An existing court order prohibiting the recognition of citizenship for the individual.
🚫
False or Incomplete Information
Providing false, misleading, or incomplete information during self-registration or any subsequent update to the Michigan Identity Registry.

Upon revocation, the individual's status is updated in the Michigan Identity Registry and they immediately lose all rights and privileges associated with citizenship of Michigan.

The Michigan Identity Registry

The Michigan Identity Registry (established under Public Act 12 — the Michigan Identity Registry Act) is the official state system that links your roleplay identity — your chosen name and date of birth — with your Roblox account.

Registration within the registry alone does not grant citizenship, residency, or any other legal status. Citizenship is a separate step that must be affirmatively requested through the petition process described above.

Who Administers the Registry?

The Secretary of State is responsible for the administration and maintenance of the Michigan Identity Registry. This is carried out through the Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records, which serves as the official registrar. The Bureau is responsible for:

📋
Review Petitions
Review all self-registrations submitted as petitions for citizenship and determine whether applicants satisfy all requirements.
✍️
Grant or Deny Citizenship
Issue decisions on citizenship applications in accordance with the Citizenship and National Status Act and other applicable law.
🔒
Maintain Secure Records
Maintain accurate and secure records of all registered citizens in the Michigan Identity Registry.
🤝
Coordinate with Other Agencies
Work with the Office of State Intelligence and the Department of Attorney General on matters related to citizenship eligibility and restricted persons.

Legal Authority

Michigan citizenship services are governed by the following legislation:

1️⃣
PA 12 — Michigan Identity Registry Act
Establishes the Michigan Identity Registry as the official record system for identity registration within the State, and designates the Secretary of State as Maintaining Officer.
2️⃣
PA 13 — Citizenship and National Status Act
Establishes the registration, recognition, and qualifications of citizens; administers the Michigan Identity Registry; identifies restricted persons; and recognizes dual citizenship.

Key Terms

Self-Registration
A voluntary registration submitted directly by an individual to the Michigan Identity Registry to establish an official identity record.
Petition for Citizenship
A designation made during self-registration indicating that the individual seeks recognition as a citizen of the state.
Platform Identifier
The permanent numerical identifier assigned to a player's Roblox account, used to link your roleplay identity in the Registry.
Restricted Persons Database
A registry of individuals barred from citizenship by court order or conviction of treason.

Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records
Department of State · Office of the Secretary of State

The Bureau serves as the official Registrar for all citizenship petitions and maintains the Michigan Identity Registry on behalf of the Secretary of State.
