Michigan State Citizenship

Learn about Michigan citizenship — what it means, who qualifies, how to apply, and how your application is processed under the Revised Citizenship Act of 2026.

What is Michigan Citizenship?

Michigan citizenship is a formal legal status granted by the State of Michigan that recognizes an individual as a citizen with all associated rights, privileges, and obligations established by law. Citizenship is administered by the Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records within the Department of State.

Once granted, citizenship is officially recorded and entitles you to all rights and privileges provided under the Revised Citizenship Act of 2026 and other applicable state law.

How to Apply for Citizenship

Citizenship is obtained by submitting an application through the official online Citizenship Application Portal maintained by the Department of State. The Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records reviews all applications within 10 days of receipt (extendable up to 15 days in certain circumstances).

1

Submit an Application via the Portal

Access the Citizenship Application Portal on the Department of State website and complete your application. The portal is the sole official method of submission — no other method of application is accepted unless otherwise provided by law.

2

Provide Required Information

Your application must include your State Identification Number (SIN), Roblox account identifier and username, current legal first and last name, date of birth, and any prior legal names. A middle name may be optionally provided.

3

Bureau Review

The Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records reviews your application within 10 days of receipt, performing identity verification, eligibility review, and record creation. The Bureau may issue a conditional or administrative exception if clarification or correction is needed, giving you 2–4 days to respond.

4

Decision & Notice

The Bureau will issue a written decision — approval, denial, or conditional exception — through the portal. If approved, citizenship is granted and officially recorded. All notices clearly state the action taken, effective date, reasons, and any required next steps.

Ready to apply? The Citizenship Application Portal is available 24/7 and is directly accessible from this page.

Required Application Information

All citizenship applications submitted through the portal must include the following information:

🪪
State Identification Number (SIN)
Your SIN assigned to you at birth pursuant to the Michigan Vital Identity and Records Act. This is required for identity verification.
🎮
Roblox Account Identifier & Username
Your Roblox account identifier and associated username. Where available, the portal may retrieve this automatically via authorized Roblox account authentication. Otherwise, you must provide it manually.
👤
Current Legal Name
Your current legal first and last name. A middle name may optionally be provided.
📅
Date of Birth
Your legally recognized date of birth.
📋
Prior Legal Names
Any prior legal names or name changes must be disclosed as part of your application.

Automated Roblox Verification: Where technically available, the portal may retrieve your Roblox account identifier and username directly from your authenticated Roblox account. Successful automated retrieval satisfies the Roblox information requirement without manual entry.

Processing Timeline

Applications are subject to the following timeline under the Revised Citizenship Act of 2026:

📬
Day of Receipt
An application is deemed received on the first calendar day following submission through the portal.
⏱️
10-Day Standard Review Period
The Bureau must take action — approve, deny, or issue a conditional/administrative exception — within 10 days of the application being deemed received.
🔄
Conditional or Administrative Exception
If additional correction or clarification is needed, the Bureau may issue a conditional exception. You will then have 2–4 days to cure the identified deficiency. Failure to respond within this period may result in denial.
📆
15-Day Maximum
The Bureau may extend the review period by up to 5 additional days where necessary. Total processing time — including any extension — shall not exceed 15 calendar days from the date of receipt.

Grounds for Denial

An application for citizenship may only be denied on the basis of a reason expressly authorized under the Revised Citizenship Act of 2026 or other applicable law. All denials must identify a specific, articulable statutory or regulatory basis and will be communicated through the portal.

🚫
Threat Screening Database
The applicant is listed in the threat screening database at a designated threat level as defined under applicable law governing that database.
🚫
Conviction of Treason
The applicant has been convicted of treason or an equivalent offense under applicable law.
🚫
False, Misleading, or Incomplete Information
The applicant has provided false, misleading, or incomplete information in any citizenship application, identity registration, or subsequent update submitted under this act.
🚫
Court Order Restricting Eligibility
A valid court order exists that expressly limits, suspends, or prohibits the applicant's eligibility to obtain citizenship under this act. Note: such orders may only be issued where expressly authorized by law — orders issued without valid legal authority are void and of no effect.

No Arbitrary Denials: No application may be denied arbitrarily, capriciously, or without reference to an identifiable provision of law. The Bureau must document the legal basis for each denial for administrative review and audit purposes.

Legal Authority

Michigan citizenship services are governed by the following legislation:

1️⃣
Bill No. 21 — Revised Citizenship Act of 2026
Establishes state citizenship and provides for its administration within the Department of State, including application procedures via the Citizenship Application Portal, eligibility standards, processing timelines, grounds for denial, enforcement, and rulemaking authority. This act repealed the prior Citizenship and National Status Act in its entirety.
2️⃣
Michigan Vital Identity and Records Act
Governs the assignment of State Identification Numbers (SINs) used as a required identifier in the citizenship application process.

Key Terms

Application
A submission for citizenship made through the Citizenship Application Portal, including all required information and communications associated with the submission.
State Identification Number (SIN)
A unique numerical identifier assigned to an individual under the Michigan Vital Identity and Records Act, required for citizenship applications.
Conditional / Administrative Exception
A temporary determination issued by the Bureau requiring correction, clarification, or additional information before a final approval or denial is issued.
Threat Screening Database
An official state-maintained database used to identify individuals who may present a security, legal, or eligibility concern relevant to citizenship determinations.
Roblox Account Identifier
The unique numerical or alphanumeric identifier assigned to a user's Roblox account, used as a required element of citizenship applications.

Bureau of Citizenship & Civil Records
Department of State · Office of the Secretary of State
