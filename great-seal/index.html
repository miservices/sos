What the Office does

The Office of the Great Seal keeps the state's official public records and applies the state's Great Seal to authenticate documents. It is part of the Bureau of Elections, and it also handled notary commissions. That program is now inactive: Michigan law, specifically 2026 PA 17, says notaries are not needed.

The Great Seal

The Great Seal is the state's official seal. When it is applied to a document, it shows that the document is authentic and came from the state. Only the Office of the Great Seal applies it.
