The fee is charged only if your filing is approved. It is added to your account when the Office approves it.

How to apply

  1. Sign in to SOS e-ServicesUse your MiGOVT account. You have to be a citizen or resident and at least 18.
  2. Open Notarial ServicesChoose Apply for a notary commission.
  3. Fill in where you liveYour county of residence and your street address. The state is always Michigan.
  4. Take the oathTick the four statements, including that you will perform the duties faithfully.
  5. Sign and submitType your full legal name as your electronic signature. It is dated the day you submit.
  6. Wait for reviewThe Office approves or denies it. You can follow it in e-Services.

When you are approved

  • You are given a commission number, like NP-482913.
  • Your commission is active for 4 years and your expiration date is recorded.
  • You can see your notary stamp in Notarial Services.
  • The $20.00 fee is charged to your account.

Renewing

The Renew button appears in Notarial Services 60 days before your commission expires. If it is approved, 4 years are added to your current expiration date. If you let it expire, you have to apply for a new commission instead.

Do not notarize with an expired commission. A notarization made without an active commission is not valid, and the Office can discipline you.

Resigning

You can resign at any time in Notarial Services. Your commission stays active until the Office approves the resignation. After that you can no longer notarize, and you can apply again later.
