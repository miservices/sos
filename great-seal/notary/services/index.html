Notary services

The notary program is inactive. Under Michigan law, specifically 2026 PA 17, notaries are not needed. The Office is not accepting notary commission applications or renewals.

Michigan law, specifically 2026 PA 17, says notaries are not needed, so the Office of the Great Seal has stopped commissioning them. Here is what you can and cannot do in Notarial Services.

ServiceStatus
Apply for a notary commissionClosed. Not accepting applications.
Renew a notary commissionClosed. Not accepting renewals.
Resign a notary commissionOpen. No fee.
See your commission and filingsOpen. Your record stays on file.

If you already hold a commission

  • Your commission stays on record until its expiration date.
  • You cannot renew it.
  • You do not need a commission to witness or sign documents, because notarization is not needed.
  • You can resign at any time in Notarial Services. Your commission stays on record until the Office approves the resignation.

Pending applications

Applications and renewals filed before the program became inactive will not be approved. They are closed without a fee.
