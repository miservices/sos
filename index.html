Your identity record lives here

The Department of State holds the vital records your MiGOVT account pulls, including your name, PIN, and date of birth. MiGOVT reads the data, but only the Department can change it. Update your name online for a fee.

How the Department is organized

Three bureaus carry out the work of the Secretary of State.

Bureau

Licensing and Regulatory Affairs

Licenses and credentials people, records, and professions.

  • Division of Professional LicensingLicenses medical and other professionals.
  • Division of Vital Records and CitizenshipCitizenship, birth certificates, and name changes.
  • Division of Driver Licensing and RecordsDriver licenses and driving records.
Bureau

Elections

Runs state and local elections, voter registration, campaign finance reporting, and compliance with election law.

  • Office of the Great SealOfficial repository of public records, notary commissions, and authentication of official documents.
Bureau

Administrative Hearings and Appeals

Holds impartial hearings and decides appeals of decisions by state agencies, counties, and municipalities. Led by the Chief Administrative Law Judge.

  • Administrative Law JudgesCan affirm, reverse, modify, or remand contested decisions.
